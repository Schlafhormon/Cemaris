Cemaris frontend

The page structure is oriented to Sunrise CMS views/_header.ejs and
views/login.ejs and the admin catalog, cemetery and burial site views,
browser handlers and dialogs at
commit 451ce034863b16502484367044f3e9b65370111a.
The application is reimplemented with React and Cemaris branding.
